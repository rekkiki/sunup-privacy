The apps I chose aren't being locked

SunUp needs Screen Time permission to shield apps. Check it is allowed in iOS Settings › Screen Time, then open SunUp › Settings and confirm "Screen Time" says Allowed. If it says Not Allowed, tap it to reopen the permission.

Can SunUp lock my whole phone?

No, and nothing on iOS can. SunUp shields the apps you pick, between your wake time and your cutoff. Calls, messages, the camera and everything you didn't pick keep working. It is a strong speed bump, not a vault.

My alarm didn't go off

On iOS 26.1 and later SunUp uses a real alarm that breaks through silent mode and Focus. Below that, iOS gives apps no way to do this, so SunUp sends a notification instead — which respects silent mode and can be missed. SunUp tells you which one you have on the wake time screen. Check notifications are allowed in SunUp › Settings.

I'm locked out and need my phone now

Open SunUp and use Emergency Access. There is a short wait, then the lock lifts. The morning is still recorded, as an unfinished one. There is also a cutoff time, after which the lock lifts by itself, so you are never shut out all day.

I paid but don't have Pro

Open SunUp › Settings › Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. That asks Apple for anything bought with your Apple Account, including on another device. If it still doesn't appear, email me with the date of purchase.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by SunUp:

  1. Open iOS Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Choose SunUp, then Cancel Subscription

Cancelling during a free trial means you are not charged. You keep access until the period you have paid for ends.

Deleting your data

Everything SunUp stores lives on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of it — your routine, your history and your streak. There is no account and nothing stored on a server. See the privacy policy.
