SunUp Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

SunUp does not collect, transmit or sell your personal information. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Everything the app knows about your mornings stays on your iPhone.

What SunUp stores, and where

Your routines, habits, wake time and completion history are stored on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere.

Apps you choose to lock

When you pick apps to shield, iOS gives SunUp an anonymous token for each one. SunUp cannot see which apps you selected, and these tokens stay on your device.

Screen Time

SunUp requests Screen Time authorization so it can shield the apps you choose. It does not read your usage data or activity reports.

Notifications and alarms

Scheduled and delivered by iOS on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. SunUp receives only whether a subscription is active. Your payment details are never visible to SunUp.

Analytics

None. SunUp includes no analytics, tracking or advertising software.

Deleting your data

Deleting SunUp removes everything it stored, including your routine and your history.

Children

SunUp is designed for adults managing their own device. It uses individual Screen Time authorization and has no parental or supervisory features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
